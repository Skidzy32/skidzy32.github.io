Jarvis

Jarvis is a private, personal assistant that runs entirely on its owner's own Windows PC. It keeps its owner's notes, shows them as a 3D map, and answers questions about them by voice or text.

What Jarvis does with Google

Jarvis can connect, read-only, to its owner's own Gmail accounts so the owner can search their mail and open messages from inside Jarvis. It uses the Gmail API with the gmail.readonly permission only. It cannot send, change, label or delete email.

Jarvis is not a hosted service and has no servers. It is a personal tool used by one person with their own accounts; it is not offered to the public.
