Jarvis privacy policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is used by one person with their own Google accounts and is not offered to anyone else.

Google user data Jarvis accesses

With the owner's permission, given on Google's own sign-in screen, Jarvis uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly to search messages and show message details (sender, recipients, subject, date and a short extract) when the owner asks.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email is fetched only when the owner searches or opens a message, and is shown on the owner's own computer. Jarvis does not copy or mirror the mailbox.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows' own data protection. They are never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Jarvis has no servers. No Google user data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Jarvis never sends, changes, labels or deletes email.
  • Email content is not passed to any AI model unless the owner explicitly turns that on for a specific account.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account in Jarvis's Settings, which revokes access at Google, or remove access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services.
